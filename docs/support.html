Before reporting a problem

  • Confirm the guess contains exactly five letters.
  • Check that each tile matches the puzzle’s gray, yellow, or green feedback.
  • Use Undo if the candidate list becomes empty after incorrect feedback.
  • Use Reset to begin a fresh solving session.
  • Include your device model and iOS or iPadOS version in the report.

About recommendations

  • H — Entropy: higher is better; it estimates information gained.
  • E — Expected Remaining: lower is better; it estimates the average number of candidates left.
  • W — Worst Bucket: lower is better; it represents the largest possible remaining group.
  • ✓ Candidate: the suggestion is still a possible answer.
