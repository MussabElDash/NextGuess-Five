Privacy Policy

NextGuess Five is designed to perform its puzzle analysis locally on your device.

Home Support

Effective date: July 26, 2026

Data collection

NextGuess Five does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Local app data

Guesses, color feedback, candidate filtering, and recommendation calculations are processed locally on your device. The app uses bundled word lists and precomputed scoring data and does not send puzzle activity to a remote server.

Clipboard

When you tap a suggested word, the app may copy that word to the system clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. The app does not read or transmit other clipboard contents.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

NextGuess Five does not use advertising networks, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or behavioral profiling.

Third-party services

The app does not rely on third-party services to provide its core puzzle-analysis features. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; visits to this website are subject to GitHub’s own privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed behavior is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, please open a privacy question on GitHub.
